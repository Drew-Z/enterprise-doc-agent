# Atlas Service Level Agreement

## Availability

The monthly service availability objective is 99.9 percent.
Scheduled maintenance is excluded only when customers receive at least 72 hours notice.

## Severity One Incidents

The initial response time for a Severity One incident is 15 minutes.
The target restoration time for a Severity One incident is four hours.

## Service Credits

When monthly availability is below 99.9 percent but at least 99.5 percent, the credit is 5 percent.
When monthly availability is below 99.5 percent but at least 99.0 percent, the credit is 10 percent.
When monthly availability is below 99.0 percent, the credit is 20 percent.
Total service credits are capped at 20 percent of the monthly fee.
