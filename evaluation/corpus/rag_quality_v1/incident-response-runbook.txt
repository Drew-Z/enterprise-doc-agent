# Orion Incident Response Runbook

## Priority One Response

The on-call engineer must acknowledge a Priority One incident within 10 minutes.
Stakeholder updates are issued every 30 minutes until service is restored.
The Site Reliability Engineering lead serves as incident commander unless explicitly delegated.

## Recovery Objectives

The recovery time objective for the customer API is two hours.
The recovery point objective for the customer API is 15 minutes.

## Security Escalation

A suspected data breach must be escalated to the Security team within 15 minutes.

## Review

A blameless post-incident review must be completed within five business days.
