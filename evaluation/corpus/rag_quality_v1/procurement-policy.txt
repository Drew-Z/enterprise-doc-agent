# Northstar Procurement Policy

## Approval Thresholds

Purchases up to USD 5,000 require approval from the requesting department manager.
Purchases from USD 5,000.01 through USD 25,000 require approval from the department director.
Purchases above USD 25,000 require approval from both the Chief Financial Officer and the Procurement Committee.

## Competitive Sourcing

Purchases above USD 10,000 require at least three written supplier quotations.
Any sole-source exception must be documented by Procurement before approval.

## Purchase Orders

A purchase order must be issued before the supplier begins work.
Invoices that do not reference an approved purchase order are returned to the requester.
