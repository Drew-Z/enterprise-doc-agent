# Redwood Employee Handbook

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 15 paid vacation days each calendar year.
Employees may carry over up to five unused vacation days, and carried days expire on March 31.

## Meals And Receipts

The reimbursable meal limit is USD 75 per day during approved business travel.
An itemized receipt is required for any individual expense above USD 25.

## Hybrid Work

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
