# Northstar Ledger Service Resilience Standard

## Service objective

The internal API availability objective is 99.7 percent for each calendar month.
That internal objective is an operating target; it does not define, calculate, or promise supplier service credits.

## Investigation record

Availability investigations record UTC timestamps, response outcomes, retry attempts, and the active deployment profile.
Planned drills, approved maintenance windows, and upstream provider events are excluded from the internal objective only when recorded under this standard.
