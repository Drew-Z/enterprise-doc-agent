# Northstar Ledger Access Governance Standard

## Requests and decisions

Any team lead may submit an access request, but a requester may not approve the request.
Restricted workspace access may be granted for no more than 30 days by the designated access owner.
As soon as a task ends or membership is deactivated, the associated access must be revoked.
Each access decision records the requester, approver, scope, decision, and UTC decision time.
